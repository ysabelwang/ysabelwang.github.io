<p>This work has two aims: (1) to accurately correct Surface Water and Ocean Topography (SWOT) altimetry measurements for internal tides using in situ observations from the mission's calibration/validation (cal/val) period in 2023, and (2) to use the corrected SWOT product to investigate submesoscale processes.</p>

<p>My progress to date addresses Objective 1. I am characterizing the behavior of internal tides at Cape Hatteras - a region where the various current systems like the Gulf Stream, fronts, eddies, and steep topography interact - using bottom-pressure and roundtrip acoustic travel time measurements from an array of current and pressure sensor equipped inverted echo sounders (<a href="https://doi.org/10.3389/fmars.2019.00232">CPIES</a>).</p>
