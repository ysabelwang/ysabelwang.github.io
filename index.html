I study the links between cross-scale ocean processes in the Northwestern Atlantic

I'm a PhD candidate in the MIT-WHOI Joint Program, advised by Magdalena Andres. My work connects well-studied large-scale, long-term, surface-expressed physical processes with the more elusive fine-scale, short-term, often subsurface ones that add up to a significant impact on the environment.

I use all available sources of information: in situ measurements, satellite imagery and model output.
